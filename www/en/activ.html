FOR FAMILIES

The Art et marges museum was approved by and for children in 2026!

What makes it great for young visitors?

  • A museum on a human scale
  • games and activities available in the exhibition for ages 4 and up, introduced by the reception staff
  • activity calendar for children as young as 6 months
  • changing table with access to a sink
  • lockers in the reception area and space for strollers
  • possibility to visit the exhibition with a stroller
  • table in the reception area for a snack

Prices, access, hours

Other kid-friendly spots near the museum:

  • Cyclup Kids, thrift store, 298 Rue Haute (3-min. walk)
  • Les Escargots Playground, 156 Rue Blaes (6-min. walk)
  • Egmont Park, 25 Boulevard de Waterloo (17-minute walk)
  • La Fabrique en Ville, restaurant, 44 Boulevard de Waterloo - in Egmont Park (17-minute walk)
