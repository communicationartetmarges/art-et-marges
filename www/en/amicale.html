BECOME A MEMBER OF THE AMICALE

Early bird! Pay your 2027 membership dues by November 10 and take part in an exclusive visit to the special exhibition marking the 40th anniversary of the Art et marges museum: Célébration.

Member of the Amicale

  • Free admission to the Art et marges museum
  • Free participation in the creative workshops organized at the Art et marges museum
  • 20% discount on guided tours
  • 20% discount on publications and merchandise published by the Art et marges museum
  • Invitation to the VIP opening reception for the “Célébration” exhibition on Thursday, November 19, 2026 (early bird)

50 euros (80 euros family fee: 2 adults and up to 2 children under 18)

Supporting Member

  • All of the above benefits +
  • A book on Art Brut, to be chosen from a selection
  • Free admission to one exclusive event per year (next event: poetry banquet on February 5, 2027)
  • Invitation to the preview of the “Célébration” exhibition on Wednesday, November 18, 2026 (early bird)

150 euros.

Benefactors

  • All of the above benefits +
  • Free admission to all activities organized by the Art et marges museum
  • A private guided tour for you and your guests, followed by a drink (up to 20 people)

500 euros.

To become a member

Please make a bank transfer to BE94 0682 0347 5614 with the following reference: “Membership Fee [Year]”—and at the same time, send an email with your contact information (and the book you’ve chosen if relevant) to florence.corna@artetmarges.be (subject line: Membership Fee [Year])

MAKE A DONATION

You can also support us by making a donation. Our organization is recognized as a tax-deductible charity, so donations of 40 € or more are tax-deductible.

Please transfer your donation to the same account number (BE94 0682 0347 5614) with the reference “Donation [year],” along with your National Registry number (required since 2024 to qualify for this tax deduction).
